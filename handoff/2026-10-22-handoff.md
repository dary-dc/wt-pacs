## 5. Lessons

* Auto mode refuses a merge without the owner's literal "merge #N", even after a "yes" to the decision behind it.
* A routine into an existing cloud session: `persist_session: true`, `persistent_session_id`, no sources (copy
  `trig_01M3asvQg6r8vxwEM93Td3uM`).
* `SendMessage` to the cloud peer "Cloud orchestrator handoff" did not reach the metadata session; its log showed
  nothing. Use a session-bound routine to ask a cloud session a question.
* A `--quick` workstation run (one round) prints "n/a (n=1)" for every paired comparison: plan 12 rounds for a verdict.
