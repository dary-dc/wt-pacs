## What the round found, in short

* **Everything earlier is finished**; the audits (165 codecs, 166 everything else: `docs/codecs/README.md` §Tried and
  open, `docs/levers.md`) class every lever as held, reopened, never tried or the owner's.
* **The link band moved** (row 162): phones reach ~100 Mbit/s median with 0.7–0.8 s loaded RTT. Decode and server CPU
  now matter more than bytes; row 167 re-derived the eight verdicts that rested on the old band (two flip).
* **Decode time is near its floor** for HTJ2K in WASM: the block decoder is 55–70 % of a frame and already SIMD; the
  big levers (code-block pool, tiles, GPU) cut one frame's latency, not total work. What is left for total time is
  small and in row 178 (OpenJPH under emscripten 6.0.x, ×0.89–0.93 on projections; the shared-heap copy, RGB ×0.85–0.95)
  and row 179 (a SIMD128 XXH3, ≤ 6 % of a mammogram).
* **AV1** is built end to end (the client picks the decoder by the series' codec; the top-bits split through
  WebCodecs is 0.32–0.50 of dav1d-WASM's decode, adopted), but the per-series rule (row 77) packs every series as
  HTJ2K: AV1 still decodes at 1.6–4.1× HTJ2K, wins only where the wire is the clock (0.89–0.98) and loses where decode
  is (1.02–1.69; Firefox 1.33–2.53). Serving AV1 by link or client stays the owner's open call.
* **An ask during a fill** waits behind the fill's written-ahead bytes (row 167: 0.49 s at 103 Mbit/s). Capping the
  send window at cwnd + 2 frames (row 171) cuts the ask 39–67 % on short queues but slows the fill 5–11 % at 103
  Mbit/s: a trade-off. Row 174 found `per-frame` streams the worst design for it; row 180 measures the four stream
  designs.
* **The writer** (rows 168, 172): 8 KiB writes make 5 page-cache folios a frame against ~1 for 1 MiB; on ext4 the hit is
  0.62–0.83×, inside the A/A resolution, so not adopted; the fill's `WILLNEED` is the larger lever (WILLNEEDFOLIO).
* **The screen** (row 169): frames reach it as 8 bits after the window, sRGB, with no presentation LUT or ICC; reading
  those tags is net where present, more than 8 bits is a trade-off.
