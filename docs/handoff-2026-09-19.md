## 5 · What cost time here, so it does not cost it twice

* **A relay that reads one datagram per wakeup drops bursts.** The kernel's receive buffer
  overflows and the loss looks like the model's. Drain the socket to `EWOULDBLOCK`, and raise
  `SO_RCVBUF`.
* **A closed-loop probe can only lose one packet per outage** — it then waits on its own timeout.
  Anything measuring loss or a blackout has to be open loop.
* **Twenty-four samples are not a tail.** S10's p99 reads 415 ms at 24 connects and 1 335 ms at
  200, and the whole finding is in the difference.
* **A ratio at one delay is not a round-trip count.** Fit the milestone against two or three round
  trips instead; the slope is the count and the relay's floor falls into the intercept.
* **`read -r a b c` puts fields 3, 4 and 5 into `c`.** Adding a column to a probe silently
  corrupts every caller that did not count.
* **The last session's close is still in the relay's delay queue** when the probe exits, so a
  script that kills the relay immediately never sees the server's own `session path` line.
* **`pack-study` wants `%03d` frame names.** `%04d` fails with a bare `stat` error.
* **Prerender needs a visible tab.** The API is present in headless Chromium and never fires.
* Everything in [`handoff-2026-09-16.md`](handoff-2026-09-16.md) §3 and §4 still holds.
