## 6 · Rules the orchestration learned, added today

* **A fold reuses the resolutions already recorded.** Where a later branch had merged an earlier one (`core-m5` over
  `frame-memory-m5-run`, `core-exp1` over `vw-exp-1-review`), its merge commit settled the conflict once, and taking it
  kept the two branches from disagreeing.
* **A shared lab driver changed under a running run** does not change that run, which drives from its own checkout.
  Its rows say which driver made them, and the reader checks that.
