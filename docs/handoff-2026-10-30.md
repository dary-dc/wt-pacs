## 5 · Rules the orchestration learned

* **Pace by the 5-hour window.** Long runs start at a window's start; about four heavy sessions a window; push every
  round. S7 died mid-run and pushed nothing.
* **A session's whole scope goes in its first prompt.** Sessions refuse scope relayed from another session (#250's
  and #251's did); to change scope, start a new one.
* **No questions from unattended sessions:** the conservative choice, recorded, and on.
* **Raw rows go to a tracked path,** never a git-ignored `work/`.
* **Decoders:** until #133 lands, copy `client/decode/wasm/built` from `claude/decoders-committed` and check
  `manifest.sha256`; Docker Hub rate-limits the build.
* **Firefox** installs from Playwright and runs headed under `xvfb-run`; headless Firefox has no WebGL2 in a worker.
