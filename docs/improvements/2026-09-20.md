## Fourth identification round, 2026-09-20 — for priority, not for novelty

**Why a fourth round.** Three things reopened closed areas: the fill turned out decoder-bound on the
comparison's cine, the paint floor was measured ([`../paint-floor.md`](../paint-floor.md)), and a
private viewer rig adopted the downloader. Eight read-only investigators, fresh, on cheaper models,
~1.0 M tokens.

**Every row below has since been measured, and each is corrected in place where the measurement
disagreed with the estimate.** The round is kept because what it ranked is what got run.

**What it changed.**
* **Decode is the clock on one content only.** On an 87-frame 8-bit colour cine over loopback the
  fill carries a ~410 ms decode tail (80 % of it). On a 237-frame 16-bit series, measured on a
  private viewer rig with the same client, the fill ends 15 ms after the last byte: decode hides
  inside the receive. On the target link both are bytes. Every decode verdict in
  [`../decode/README.md`](../decode/README.md) was ranked where decode is the fill; they hold for
  that cell, for one ask, and for the fast end of the target link — not for a long 16-bit fill.
* **The vsync rule.** On a 60 Hz display a change to one ask moves the median only when it crosses a
  16.7 ms line. A sub-vsync shave buys tail margin, not median. Rank sub-vsync decode and paint
  levers under anything that crosses a line.
* **The first ask's defaults are undecided.** Both levers are now measured and still undecided — the
  numbers are below, and the choice is the owner's.

the window.
