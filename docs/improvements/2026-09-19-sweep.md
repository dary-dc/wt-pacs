# Improvements — second identification sweep, 2026-09-19: what the shaped link and the desktop still hide

**Branch:** `claude/serene-rubin-wakfg7` · **Status:** identified; **S26, S28, S30–S33 and S37–S39 were then
measured the same day on local lanes and are corrected in place below — everything else is unmeasured
and uncoded**. Every line is read from source (this tree, the QUIC, TLS and WebTransport crates in
`Cargo.lock`, Chromium, WebKit), from specs, papers and bug trackers, or is arithmetic; a line that
could not be checked against a primary source says **unverified**. Front door:
[`README.md`](README.md). Method: [`../identification-sweep.md`](../identification-sweep.md).
Findings continue the first sweep's numbering ([`2026-09-18.md`](2026-09-18.md), S1–S22).

**Why now.** Every row queued from the first sweep is done. Its results reopened three areas: the
impaired link became the instrument everything stands on, so *its* model is now a regime; one
measurement (an outage) has a number and no mechanism; and the third-party SDK became modifiable,
so the page half of the clock is no longer closed. Eleven areas were swept; two came back with
nothing new and say so in §8.

**Target arithmetic:** 20 Mbit, 60–80 ms round trip, as before.

setter.

## 3 · The idle ask on a sleeping radio

Goal 2 is "one frame asked on an idle session". On every rig an idle session is free. No doc on
any branch mentions radio resource states.

| # | Finding | Size | Decided by |
| - | ------- | ---- | ---------- |
| **S34** | **Every measured carrier drops the radio to idle after 5–10.5 s without traffic, and promotion back costs 190–396 ms on 4G and 341–1 907 ms on 5G** (a 2021 measurement study, its table of inactivity timers). Chromium's own session ping is every 15 s and the keep-alive `transport/adr-idle-sessions.md` proposes is 20 s — chosen against the idle *timeout*, and both longer than every timer measured. So each idle ask pays a full promotion that no measurement here has counted | 2–20× the whole idle-ask figure | a device with a SIM. A container can only show how the stack reacts to a first packet delayed by that much (spurious client probes, an inflated round-trip sample) — an idle-penalty lever on the relay |
| **S35** | **Hold the radio promoted:** traffic more often than the shortest timer (~5 s). Connected-mode radio power is ~3× idle-mode (a 2012 LTE power study) | removes S34 entirely, at a battery cost for the life of every idle session | a device: drain at 3 / 5 / 10 s against today's cadence |
| **S36** | **Promote at intent:** a minimal uplink packet on `pointerdown` / `touchstart`, so promotion overlaps the gesture instead of preceding the ask. Same events as the session-survival triggers, different purpose | up to S34's whole cost when touch-to-ask exceeds it; the gap is unmeasured | a device |

**Dropped:** Wi-Fi power save with the viewer in the foreground — driver source shows aggressive
skipping engages only on screen-off; a foreground app pays ~124 ms at worst.

## 6 · Reconnects, and the decoder's second visit

| # | Finding | Decided by |
| - | ------- | ---------- |
| **S41** | **Careful Resume** (IETF tsvwg; cited to us as RFC 9959 — number unverified): save a client's window and minimum round trip, start its next connection in an unvalidated phase with a larger window, retreat at the first sign of congestion. It is the per-client form of W1's 32-packet window, aimed at the gap W1 measured (5.8 round trips fresh against 1.3 warmed) on exactly the path S2, S3 and S30 make routine. **Reachability is open:** the controller factory is not told the peer, and `wtransport` 0.7.2's `IncomingSession` wraps `quinn::Incoming` privately, so `accept_with` is not reachable — S22's verdict stands. ~180 ms per reconnect if half the gap is recovered. **Answered 2026-09-24 (C1): reachable through the settings-early patch's `accept_with`, keyed on a server-issued token rather than the address; not built — at 80 ms the push at open recovers what the jump does (130 against 134 ms of 462), [`../proposal-careful-resume.md`](../proposal-careful-resume.md)** | a proposal first (structural), then `first_ask` cells |

## 8 · Looked at and dropped — do not re-look

Application FEC and hedged asks (pay only above ~2× RTT of budget with independent loss; the
redundancy competes with the frame); multipath QUIC (no browser exposes it); warming at hover
(subsumed by S20); repeat-open storage (already `client-shape-plan.md` M5); the network-information
hint as a window seed (Android only, ≤ 2 round trips, coarse); relaxed SIMD (the block decoder's hot
loops are bitwise, and it is Chrome-only); memory bandwidth (0.1–0.3 ms a frame); SIMD128 → NEON
lowering (no published penalty); `async` / `defer` on a large classic script (reorders, does not
remove the compile); COOP/COEP and dedicated workers as back/forward-cache blockers (not in
Chromium's list); OCSP stapling (adds bytes to S37's flight); Early Hints (nginx has none, and R2's
hints are already inline); Retry to lift the amplification limit (costs the round trip it would
save); cheaper `postMessage` shapes, `MessageChannel` against `Worker.postMessage`,
`scheduler.postTask` for ingest (S44: not the binder, and no iOS arm); the ACK-frequency
`reordering_threshold` (asks the peer; Chromium does not negotiate it); WebGPU for paint (nothing
WebGL2 lacks here); WebKit's canvas area and texture limits (12 Mpx fits); capping the probe-timeout
backoff (no setter, ≤ 0.5 s). **Two areas came back without a lever:** decode on phone silicon and
the phone's memory ceiling produced caveats for a device (S46), nothing a container or a change can
act on.
