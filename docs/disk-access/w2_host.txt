Chosen: --stride 500000 at every size. It is the smallest value clearing the 0.95 floor at
all four, and holding it constant is what makes the size curve interpretable -- if stride
moved with size, the curve would confound the two. A contiguous stride is a hit cell wearing
a cold label at every size (1.6-16.4 % miss), as on every host so far.

--- what could not be measured here ---
RLIMIT_MEMLOCK is 8192 KiB, hard, no root. The lab ring arms register readers x depth x size
of buffers, so hybrid_lazyring at 64 readers x 250 kB needs 15.3 MiB and fails outright:
"register_buffers: Cannot allocate memory (os error 12)". That cell carries product only.
The shipped ReadCtx registers no buffers and falls back to the pool on refusal, so the
product is not subject to this ceiling -- the lab arm is.

--- an instrument defect found and controlled, affecting w1 ---
--monitors 1 counts the co-tenant monitor's own CPU in cpu_ns_per_ask (documented: "0
disables and removes their CPU from the totals"). On a warm cell finishing in milliseconds
that is a large, intermittent addend. Interleaved control, 12 rounds, 250 kB warm depth 1
(w2_monitor_control.tsv):

    monitors 0   product 38.7-44.6   lazyring 36.2-43.4   pool 37.0-40.6   0 outliers each
    monitors 1   product 38.7-129.0  lazyring 35.1-129.3  pool 34.4-101.8  2-3 of 12 each

Every warm CPU number in w1_*_arms.tsv was taken with --monitors 1 and carries this. The
cold cells do not: their spread is tight (product 1279-1390 ns/ask at 250 kB depth 1).
