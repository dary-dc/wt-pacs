--- caveat: btrfs transparent compression is ON (compress=zstd:1) ---
The fixture body is a single repeated byte (0xAB), so it is pathologically
compressible in a way real frame data is not. filefrag reports every extent
as 'encoded' (= compressed) and the physical offsets are non-sequential:
 ext:     logical_offset:        physical_offset: length:   expected: flags:
   0:        0..      31:     675555..    675586:     32:             encoded
   1:       32..      63:     664783..    664814:     32:     675587: encoded
   2:       64..      95:     664968..    664999:     32:     664815: encoded
   3:       96..     127:     665043..    665074:     32:     665000: encoded
Consequence: cold reads move far fewer physical bytes here than on the lab's
ext4, and pay zstd decompression instead. Treat section-3 magnitudes and the
cold/miss regimes as host-specific. The hop tax (section 1) is measured on
warm, page-cache-resident cells and is not affected by either.
