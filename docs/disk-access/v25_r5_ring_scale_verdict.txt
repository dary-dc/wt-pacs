R5 — does one ring per session reintroduce threads at scale?

The risk as written: 'Enough concurrent rings would force io-wq punting and could
reintroduce threads — the exact property claim 4 rests on.'

Cell: 84 MB study under a 64 MB cgroup memory cap so misses stay high as readers rise
(failcnt 2.86M / 2.75M — the cap bound hard). Cold, depth 4, 256 asks, 5 repeats,
two runs of the same configuration. `threads` is /proc/self/status, which counts
io-wq workers: if the ring punts, they appear here.

* `hybrid` and `uring` hold thread count at 5 at every reader count, in both runs,
  with 50-80% of reads missing. **No io-wq punting appeared at 128 concurrent rings.**
* `pool` is the arm that grows threads: it is the blocking pool doing what it is for.
* Thread count is a structural difference, not a percentage: it needs no drift rule.
* CPU/ask under this much pressure is noisy and is NOT quoted as a result here; the
  claim is about thread count only.
