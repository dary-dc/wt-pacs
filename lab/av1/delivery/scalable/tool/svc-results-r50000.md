# SVC metrics at 50 Mbit/s (2026-11-04)

What ran: three breast tomosynthesis volumes filled end to end through the server, a 50 Mbit/s link and a browser, 13 ways of coding each, at normal and 4×-slowed CPU, 10 rounds.
What it shows: a scalable AV1 preview puts a picture on every slice in 0.3–0.5 s (0.5–0.9 s at 4×) where the exact image takes 11–18 s, and at 1× costs the exact fill what it did at 20 Mbit/s.
What it changes from 20 Mbit/s: at 4× the browser's decode, not the link, sets when the last exact AV1 frame lands, so the layered variants lose 3–92 % to the shipped payload there.
What it rests on: the three DBT volumes `dbts_a1`, `dbts_b1`, `dbts_c1` only, on one 4-vCPU host; every exact frame matched its checksum and every preview native dav1d.
What is left: part 5's rule (another session applies it once every link is in), 5 Mbit/s, loss and jitter (other sessions), and the breast ultrasound and ABUS rows.

**Terms.** The protocol is [`docs/av1/README.md`](../../../../../docs/av1/README.md) §The scalable preview as a tool, 10;
how each number is made is [`README.md`](README.md) §SVC metrics, and this file keeps the first sitting's method
([`svc-results.md`](svc-results.md)), changing only the link. A *fill* is one whole series fetched and decoded in a
fresh browser; *1×* and *4×* are the browser's CPU unthrottled and slowed four times (`lab/scripts/cpu_throttle.mjs`).
*`k2`* is the shipped single-layer AV1 payload; the *base* is a quarter-size lossy picture, the *middle* a full-size
lossy one, and the *exact* layer the lossless frame. *t_X* is the time from the fill's issue until every slice is
exact. A *`VOID`* visit is one where the relay's own timing broke the rig's 1 ms bound; it is dropped and counted. The
*A/A* arm is HTJ2K filled twice, and its spread is the smallest difference reported. MB is 10⁶ bytes.

**What ran.** `dbts_a1` (56 × 1142×1785, 12-bit), `dbts_b1` (50 × 2560×3328, 10-bit), `dbts_c1` (69 × 976×2513,
12-bit) × `r50000` (50 Mbit/s, 20 ms each way, the relay's 200-packet queue) × 1× and 4× × the 13 variants, layer-major,
cells and variants in a Williams order, a fresh server, relay and browser a visit, headless Chromium 153.0.8010.12,
rounds 0–9 whole: 780 visits, 11 `VOID` dropped, 769 kept, n = 8–10 a cell and variant. Rows:
[`svc-rows-r50000.jsonl`](svc-rows-r50000.jsonl). Rebuilt in a fresh container from the pinned sources; every coding
came out byte-identical to the first sitting's ([`svc-shapes.tsv`](svc-shapes.tsv)), so tables 3 (layer error) and
5 offline are the first sitting's and are not repeated. No Firefox round here: the protocol's one ran in the first sitting.

**Host, and where it saturates.** 4 vCPU Xeon at 2.1 GHz, 15 GB; browser and server on three cores, the relay alone on
the fourth. The wire never saturates: every exact fill arrived at 6.02–6.07 MB/s, 96–97 % of the link's 6.25 MB/s,
against the ~45 MB/s at which the first sitting found the relay saturating, and 11 of 780 visits were `VOID`. At 1×
the link is the clock: the last exact frame lands 0.0–0.4 s after the last byte in every cell. **At 4× the browser's
three slowed cores saturate on AV1 decode**: `k2`'s last exact frame trails its last byte by 0.3–2.0 s, the
two-layer variants' by 0.9–6.1 s and the three-layer ones' by 2.7–17.6 s (the most on `dbts_b1`), while HTJ2K stays within
0.3 s. So the 4× rows' t_X and fill times measure this host's decode with the layers competing for it, not the link,
and nothing is claimed for them past this host (*derived* from tables 4 and 7).

**Exactness**, every visit, `VOID` ones included: 38 500/38 500 exact frames against their checksums; 31 500/31 500
bases and 14 000/14 000 middles equal to native dav1d at their operating point; no preview after its exact picture, no
stray, no stall-flush. **Mutated first**, in this build, at `r50000` 1× ([`svc-mutations-r50000.jsonl`](svc-mutations-r50000.jsonl)):
a sample flipped and a truth flipped turned every exact frame, base and middle on `dbts_a1` to 0; a middle posted as
exact gave 56 and 50 strays a visit; the base taken as the frame gave 0 exact, refused by name on `dbts_a1` and caught
by the hash on `dbts_b1`; the middle taken as the frame gave 0 exact. `stallMs` back to 2 000 ms on `dbts_b1` at 4×
again did not fire: 150/150 exact (`p2-q20`, `p2-q40`, `p3-m20`, one visit each), as in the first sitting.
