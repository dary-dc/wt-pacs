# SVC metrics — 20 Mbit/s with 2 % loss (2026-11-07, in progress)

What was run: the pre-registered SVC metrics protocol on one more link, 20 Mbit/s with 2 % random loss each way, on the three DBT volumes `dbts_a1`, `dbts_b1` and `dbts_c1`, 13 variants, at 1× and 4× CPU; 0 of 10 whole rounds so far.
What it shows: see the tables; the reading is written into [`svc-rule.md`](svc-rule.md) when part 5's rule is applied.
What it rests on: three digital breast tomosynthesis (DBT) volumes only (`dbts_a1` 56 × 1142×1785 12-bit, `dbts_b1` 50 × 2560×3328 10-bit, `dbts_c1` 69 × 976×2513 12-bit; EA1141); no breast ultrasound cine or ABUS exists in the fixtures. One 4-vCPU container and headless Chromium 153.0.8010.12. Nothing is claimed for any other series, modality, host, browser or loss pattern.
What is left: the remaining rounds of this link if not 10; the breast ultrasound cine and ABUS rows.

**Terms.** *SVC*, scalable coding: one AV1 stream coded in layers: a quarter-size (¼ of each side) lossy **base**, in `p3-*` a full-size lossy **middle**, and the **exact** (lossless) top. *Layer-major*: every slice's base is sent first, then every middle, then every exact frame. *`k2`*: the shipped lossless payload, single-layer AV1 split on 12 bits at k = 2; *`htj2k`*: HTJ2K, exact; *`res`*: HTJ2K's half-size prefix first; *`a2-*`*: a base sent beside `k2`; *`p2-*`*, *`p3-*`*: two or three layers with the exact top predicted from the layer below; *`lossy-*`*: the lossy layers alone, nothing exact. *t_P* / *t_ℓ,all*: seconds from the fill's issue until every slice has a picture at layer ℓ or better; *t_X*: until every slice is exact. *`VOID`*: a visit dropped because the relay's own timing broke the rig's 1 ms bound (p99). *A/A*: `htj2k-aa`, the same files as `htj2k` again. The full protocol is [`docs/av1/README.md`](../../../../../docs/av1/README.md) §The scalable preview as a tool, 10; how each number is made, [`README.md`](README.md) §SVC metrics.

## Method card

* **Input.** `dbts_a1`, `dbts_b1`, `dbts_c1` as `lab/av1/fetch_data.sh` fetches them; the branch at commit `7aa80e17`, rebuilt in a fresh container on 2026-11-07. The rebuilt `svc-shapes.tsv` (every shape's bytes, exact-frame count and layer error) is byte-identical to the committed one.
* **Encode.** The commands of [`README.md`](README.md) §SVC metrics, unchanged: `k2` by `split-rule/make_frames.py --preset allintra:7 --k 2` (libaom 3.15.1 `aomenc`); the layered shapes by `shapes.py --shapes two-quarter-q20,two-quarter-q40,three-full-m10,three-full-m20,three-full-m30` (libaom 3.15.1's `svc_encoder_rtc` with `encoder/svc_encoder_rtc.patch`); HTJ2K and its prefix by `resolution-level/make_frames.py --frames 100 --screen 850` and `prefix.mjs` (OpenJPH 0.31.0); the fill's series by the tool's `make_frames.py` with the SVC metrics flags.
* **Decoders.** In the page: dav1d 1.5.4 (commit `54706fc6`) built to WASM SIMD with Emscripten 3.1.74 plus `two-layer/dav1d_wrap_op.c`'s operating-point control (`simd-op.wasm`, SHA-256 `9415844f…`) for every layered variant, the worker [`decoder.js`](decoder.js) as committed, 10 000 ms stall bound; `k2` through the product's worker; HTJ2K through `@cornerstonejs/codec-openjph` as `fetch_openjph.sh` pins it. Checks against native dav1d 1.5.4.
* **Run.** `run.mjs --frames lab/.av1-work/preview-e2e --links r20000 --impairs l2 --throttles 1,4 --variants htj2k,htj2k-aa,res,k2,a2-q20,a2-q40,p2-q20,p2-q40,p3-m10,p3-m20,p3-m30,lossy-q20,lossy-m20 --per-frame --rounds 1 --first-round R`, one process a round, its temporary directory removed after the round. Cells and the variants inside each in `run.mjs`'s Williams order (`lab/order.mjs`), a fresh browser a visit. A round cut short is set aside whole and run again.
* **Clock.** From the fill's issue in the page to the event named (first picture, every slice pictured, every slice exact), page-side `performance.now()`.
* **Bytes.** `wireBytes`: the length of every unit the page's worker received (exact frames and previews, so a base sent three times counts three times), QUIC framing and retransmissions not counted; the bytes test divides by `k2`'s on the same series. The *wire rate* below is these bits ÷ the last byte's time, a goodput.
* **Link.** `lab/scripts/link_impair.py` in userspace (no `tc` in the container): 20 Mbit/s each way through a 200-packet tail-drop queue, 20 ms one way, `--loss 2`: each UDP datagram dropped independently with probability 0.02, in both directions, before the queue, the relay seeded with the round number. Real loss's bursts are not modelled.
* **Host.** 4 vCPU Xeon, 15 GB; the browser and server on three cores, the relay alone on the fourth (`chrt -f 50`). The clock reported at each round's start: round 0 2.10 GHz.

**Where the host saturates.** Recomputed from the raw rows below at every push, over every visit with an exact layer, `VOID` ones included: wire bits ÷ the last byte's time; the last exact frame against the last byte; the median t_X at 4× ÷ at 1× per series and variant.

*No round complete yet: the harness started at 2026-11-07 22:04 UTC.* One early observation, not a result: at 2 % loss  on  at 1× took 249.5 s to its last byte, against 28–30 s on the clean 20 Mbit/s link, so a round here takes hours.



**Exactness**, over every visit, `VOID` ones included: every exact frame against its checksum, every base and middle against native dav1d at its operating point, previews after their exact picture (`late`), lossy layers posted as exact (`stray`), and stall-flushes. The checks are the first sitting's, each mutated there ([`svc-mutations.jsonl`](svc-mutations.jsonl)); this sitting adds no check and so mutates none. *Not yet counted.*
